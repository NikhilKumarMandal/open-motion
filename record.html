Record your browser in four steps.

Pick a screen, add your camera or mic if you want, and hit record. Zooms and pans are added for you afterwards.

  1. 01

    Share this screen

    Select the screen showing this browser window. Entire screen gives the best results.

    To record sound, tick Share tab audio or Share system audio in the sharing dialog. Otherwise the recording is video only.

    System audio turns off your microphone for that recording. If you need both, use tab audio instead.

    Screen selected - Ready to record
  2. 02

    Camera and microphoneoptional

    The camera adds a picture-in-picture overlay. The microphone mixes your voice with the screen audio. Each asks for its own permission.

  3. 03

    Hide the sharing bar

    Chrome's "sharing this screen" bar will show up in the recording unless you hide it. If it's on another display, ignore it.

  4. 04

    Start recording

    You'll jump back to your previous tab, see a countdown, and recording starts. Stop it from the toolbar button or this tab.
